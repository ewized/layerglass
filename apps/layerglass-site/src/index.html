AT HOME ON YOUR NETWORK

A small container.
A better view.

Run the Python backend with Docker on hardware you control. It discovers approved printers, keeps their access codes locally, and relays the camera to your browser.

The hosted demo uses sample footage. Connecting to a real printer uses your local backend; it does not send your printer feed through our website.

  1. 1

    Start LayerGlass

    Build and run the Docker container on your server, NAS or other Docker host.

  2. 2

    Find your printer

    Discover it on your LAN and save its own access code in Settings → Printers.

  3. 3

    Watch from your browser

    Open the viewer on your phone or desktop. Your local setup works without a cloud account.

AN EARLY PRODUCT, BUILT IN THE OPEN

Focused on the view.

Bambu Lab is the first supported manufacturer. Other manufacturers need an approved adapter; generic camera streams are not added as printers.

Printer controls, accounts and recording entire print jobs are possibilities on the roadmap. Today's app focuses on live viewing, session replay and read-only telemetry.
